MAZE WEBSITE UPDATE

Included files:
- index.html
- css/styles.css
- js/main.js
- js/archive.js
- js/projects.js

HOW TO APPLY
1. Download and unzip this package.
2. In your GitHub repository, replace the matching files with these versions:
   index.html
   css/styles.css
   js/main.js
   js/archive.js
   js/projects.js
3. Do NOT delete or replace your existing assets/ folder, privacy.html, accessibility.html, or other existing files.
4. Commit and push the changes. GitHub Pages will publish the update.

NOTES
- The color palette, typography direction, navigation and PATHS section are preserved.
- The ARCHIVE section keeps its existing visual structure and filter controls.
- The ENTRY reveal radius is reduced further (72px on mobile / 118px on desktop).
- FIX THIS now uses three short, customer-first activities in plain Hebrew: what a customer wants to know, which ad is clearer, and what action they want to take.
- CONTACT keeps the form on the left and service list on the right on desktop. On mobile and narrow screens, the service list appears first and the form sits directly below it.
- The WhatsApp number remains the original placeholder. Until you replace it in js/main.js, form submission opens an email draft to mazebyme@gmail.com.
- Existing image paths and logo paths are expected to remain in your repository.
